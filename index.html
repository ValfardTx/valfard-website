Our Technology

Enzyme-responsive controlled
release of TGF‑β

Diabetic foot ulcers are starved of the growth factors needed to rebuild tissue. At the same time, the wound bed is flooded with destructive enzymes that break down the few reparative signals still present — so the critical mediators of wound remodelling never survive long enough to act.

Valfard’s enzymatically crosslinked polymer-peptide hydrogel carries TGF‑β through peptide linkers engineered to respond to exactly that signal. When MMP‑9 is present, it breaks down the crosslinked gel and actively releases TGF‑β locally — right where the wound needs it — promoting wound remodelling while the hydrogel keeps the wound bed moist throughout.

How the therapy works, step by step
